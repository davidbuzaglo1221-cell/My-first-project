# David's brand

## How to work with David (the most important rule)
- **Learn from him, don't echo him.** When he explains his attitude ("I don't follow the herd, I create it"), that is the brief, not the copy. Never paste his own sentences back onto the screen. Show the attitude through the work: the look, the confidence, what is left out.
- Examples he sends are the standard to study and exceed, in our own original way. Never copy the reference's photos, name or layouts one to one.

## What the reference teaches (@samrascals_, UK barber, 1.7M)
- **The person is the brand.** Faces, the barber at work, the clients' cuts, his lifestyle (city, car, workspace). No graphics, no templates, no text on the grid.
- **Real photography, editorial and cinematic**: tight portraits with a direct, calm gaze; profile shots that show the shape of the cut; behind-the-scenes in the chair; wide lifestyle frames with architecture.
- **Colour**: muted, desaturated, warm sepia/cream and black & white; soft contrast, film grain, no saturated colour. One palette across the whole grid, so it reads as one body of work.
- **Hair language**: textured, natural, grown-out modern men's cuts: slick-backs, curtains, mullets, textured crops and moustaches. Fades are part of it, not the headline.
- **Attitude**: effortless confidence. Never selling, never explaining, never shouting. Silence and space do the work. Minimal captions.
- **Profile**: short bio with the business handles, contact, one link, a community.

## Content pillars for David
1. **The cut**: before → after, profile and back shots, natural light, the same framing every time.
2. **The barber**: David at work, hands, tools, focus; portraits of David himself in the house style.
3. **The life**: the barbershop space, the city, the routine, training (his fitness path fits here).
4. **The proof**: clients and their reactions, real moments, no staged praise.
5. **The education**: short, calm technique clips later on, once he has an audience.

## Video in this brand
- Real footage first, graded to the house look (desaturate, warm the highlights, lift the blacks slightly, grain).
- Rhythm: fast but clean; cut on the beat of trending sounds; hold on the final look for a beat.
- On-screen text: none, or one short line in a plain lowercase-feeling sans. No exclamation marks, no emojis, no slogans.
- Graphic-only reels (reels/reel-01, reel-02) are a side format, not the main brand.

## Words
- Short, certain, understated. Say less than you could. Let the work answer.
- Captions: one line or none. Hebrew first; handles and hashtags minimal.
