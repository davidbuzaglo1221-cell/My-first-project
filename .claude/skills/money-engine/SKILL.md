# Money engine

The goal is one good opportunity that brings real money, not twenty projects.

## 1. Generate (go beyond the obvious)
Look in five areas: A. services to businesses (sites, ad videos, content, automations, booking, sales process, branding); B. digital products (templates, guides, courses, content packs, work systems for a niche); C. software (micro-SaaS, small apps, AI tools, subscription or one-time); D. digital assets (content sites, SEO assets, channels, useful databases, free tools that feed a paid product); E. new areas nobody mentioned.
Start from David's unfair advantages (`TITAN-BUSINESS-OS/BUSINESS_PROFILE.md`): inside the barber world, Hebrew market, an AI production team that costs almost nothing. Reject generic ideas ("open a YouTube channel"). Look for problems people already pay to solve.

## 2. Research (no gut feeling)
For each candidate: search the web (Firecrawl), find competitors, prices, customer reviews and complaints, services people already pay for, and whether we can be better, faster or cheaper. Every number gets a link. Log everything in `TITAN-BUSINESS-OS/RESEARCH_LOG.md` with the date.

## 3. The 10 questions
1. What problem does it solve? 2. Who is the customer? 3. Is there real demand (evidence)? 4. Who are the competitors? 5. How can we test it cheaply? 6. Time and money needed? 7. How do we get the first customers? 8. Revenue model? 9. Risks? 10. The first concrete step?

## 4. Score (1–5)
Potential (high = good) · Complexity (low = good) · Time to first income (low = good) · Startup cost (low = good).
Prefer: testable in days, sellable before building, deliverable with tools we already have (`TITAN-BUSINESS-OS/SKILLS_DIRECTORY.md`).

## 5. Output to David (Hebrew, simple)
- Up to five opportunities in a short table with scores, each with its strongest evidence link.
- ONE recommendation and why, plus the single first action (if it needs David, one step at a time).
- Update `TITAN-BUSINESS-OS/BUSINESS_OPPORTUNITIES.md`.
- Never promise income; label estimates as estimates.
