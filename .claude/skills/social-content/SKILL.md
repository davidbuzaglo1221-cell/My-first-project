# Social content standard

## Before designing
- Know the goal (bookings, followers, a sale) and the audience. If unclear, ask David one short Hebrew question with tappable options.
- Look at what the best accounts and pages in the niche do now; aim above them. Everything original — style of, never a copy of.

## Carousels (Instagram)
- 1080×1350 (4:5), 5–10 slides, one consistent grid and palette.
- Slide 1 = hook (big promise or tension, ≤ 7 words). Middle = one idea per slide, large type, a visual anchor. Last = call to action (save / share / book / DM a word).
- Hebrew RTL typography: Heebo or Assistant for punch, Frank Ruhl Libre for elegance; minimum 48 px body on canvas; strong hierarchy; plenty of air.
- Render with HTML/CSS + Playwright screenshots (or `canvas-design`), check every slide at phone size, export PNGs in order.

## Landing pages
- Hero with one clear promise, a real photo or striking visual, and the main action above the fold (WhatsApp / booking / call).
- Then: proof (reviews, before/after, numbers), services and prices, how it works in 3 steps, FAQ, final call to action. Sticky action button on mobile.
- Motion that feels premium (scroll reveals, micro-interactions), fast (Lighthouse ≥ 90), RTL, dark/light tokens, published on its own address (GitHub Pages) and verified live.

## Images
- Premium look: considered lighting, depth, texture and a limited palette; never generic stock-like art.
- Use the available image tools (Canva, Picsart, or code-generated art) and check the result at full size before delivering.

## Delivery
- Files ready to post, plus the Hebrew caption, hashtags and posting tip. Never post on his behalf without his approval.
