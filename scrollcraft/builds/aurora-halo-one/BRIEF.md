# BRIEF: AURORA · Halo One (דמו קונספט)

Source: the user's brief in the conversation. Creative decisions were given
explicitly by the user, so they are recorded here as the user's answers.

- **What it is:** a concept demo for premium headphones. The brand, product
  and copy are fictional. The page says so in the hero and the footer. It is
  not a live store, and there is no checkout or lead form.
- **Vibe:** deep black, broken white text, one violet accent, large precise
  type, lots of empty space, soft light, smooth motion. No template look, no
  emoji, no card clutter.
- **Language:** Hebrew, RTL (`lang="he" dir="rtl"`).
- **Assets:** none supplied. The headphones are hand-built SVG and CSS. No
  photos, footage, external fonts or external scripts. No AI generation.

## Feeling curve

1. Calm: a single object and one sentence.
2. Curiosity: the layers come apart slowly as you scroll.
3. Stillness: the object fades and one line remains.
4. Clarity: three details arrive one at a time.
5. Resolve: the object is whole again, and the page offers to start over.

## Peak

The quiet beat, "רגע אחד. רק שלך.", after the layers separate. It is the
largest pause on the page.

## Grammar and device

- Sections in order, with sticky stages for the pinned beats.
- Signature move: the headphones come apart in layers as you scroll, and the
  motion reverses on the way back.
- Scroll is driven by a small local script, not the skill engine. The engine
  is built for scrubbing video frames, and this demo has no video.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: the layers show as a static exploded view, and all
  text is visible without the scroll effects.
- Keyboard: links are real focusable controls with a visible focus ring.
- No horizontal scroll at phone widths.
